# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Restaurant managers and admins logging into an Arabic-language (RTL) admin panel for Beit Jedi, a restaurant management system. They are professionals who use this daily. Context: typically a desktop or laptop screen, at the start of a workday.

## Product Purpose
Beit Jedi Admin is a comprehensive restaurant management platform. The login screen is the first impression — it must feel trustworthy, clean, and immediately competent. Success: the user signs in without friction and feels confidence in the tool they are about to use.

## Brand Personality
Precise, composed, trustworthy. Not cold — refined. The brand uses navy and gold; it should feel like a well-run establishment, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references
- No split two-panel layouts with decorative circles or orbital shapes
- No glassmorphism
- No hero-metric templates
- No identical card grids
- No AI-generated aesthetic clichés (blurred blobs, gradient mesh backgrounds)
- Nothing that looks like it was designed by an AI trying to look premium

## Design Principles
1. Form follows function — the login form is the entire purpose; everything supports it
2. Restraint is strength — one accent color, used precisely, not decoratively
3. Typography earns hierarchy — size and weight contrast over decoration
4. Human over robotic — no gratuitous geometry or tech-fantasy motifs
5. RTL-native — every spacing, alignment, and reading-flow decision respects Arabic direction

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA. Full keyboard navigation. Visible focus states. Sufficient color contrast on all text.
